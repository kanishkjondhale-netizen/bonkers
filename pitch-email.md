Hi Shubham and team,

I'm going to skip the pitch deck — you can just try the thing.

**https://kanishkjondhale-netizen.github.io/bonkers/**

Open that on your phone. Point the camera at a hangtag barcode, watch it drop into the bag,
pay by UPI, and walk out showing an exit pass at the door. No app to download, no account to
create, no counter. It's built in BONKERS' own colours because I wanted to see it *as*
BONKERS, not as a generic demo.

Here's why I got obsessed with this:

**The queue is where you lose money.** Every minute someone stands in line is a minute they
reconsider the second item in their hand. Removing the line isn't a convenience feature — it's
a conversion feature.

**No download, no signup.** It runs in the browser. A customer goes from "curious" to
"scanning" in one tap. Every app-install requirement you remove is a wall of drop-off removed.

**Your staff stop being cashiers.** Nobody is stuck behind a counter ringing up socks. They're
on the floor styling people and selling — which is the only thing a physical store does that
the internet can't.

**Built for how India actually pays.** UPI-native. No card terminal, no rental hardware, no
new POS contract. The customer pays into your VPA from the app they already use.

**Zero hardware to buy.** The scanner is the customer's phone. The barcodes are the tags you
already print. A pilot costs you a weekend, not a purchase order.

**The Vault is the real long game.** Everything a customer buys stays in their Vault after
they leave — with Resell and Repair sitting right there. That turns a one-time transaction
into an ongoing relationship, and quietly sets BONKERS up for resale and circularity before
the rest of the market gets there.

**You learn what nearly sold.** Scans that never make it to checkout are the most honest
demand data in retail — the stuff a normal POS never sees.

Straight with you: this is a working prototype, not a finished product. The scanning, the bag,
the UPI hand-off and the exit pass are all real and running. Payment *confirmation* is still
manual — production needs a Razorpay/Cashfree webhook and a server-signed door pass, which is
about two weeks of work, not two quarters.

I'd love 15 minutes to show you where this goes, and I'd happily run it live in one store for
a weekend so you can watch real customers use it.

Either way — try the link. It's more fun than reading about it.
